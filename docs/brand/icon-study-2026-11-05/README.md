# Face-on-paper icon study · November 5, 2026

**Selected: B — raised-eyebrow smirk**, explicitly chosen by the owner in chat.
The closer face and lined-paper background follow the requested direction.

- A: familiar side-eye, closest to the previous expression.
- B: raised eyebrow and closed-beak smirk; production asset source.
- C: open-beak grin.

[Comparison with 60px light/dark search-row mockups](comparison.png).
All three concepts are 1024 × 1024 RGB PNGs, opaque, with square corners.
The mockups show a generic platform corner mask; they are not live App Store
screenshots or evidence of conversion improvement.

Generated through Sume on November 5, 2026 using GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst,
high quality, 1K, 1:1 and opaque background. Each concept used the previous
Schwanki iOS icon as the reference. Prompts kept the white tufted goose, orange
beak, pink cheek and heavy outlines; removed the notebook, lettering and jacket;
and requested an oversized face over faint blue rules and a red top-margin rule.
Only the expression and head angle differ between concepts. Quoted generation
cost was $0.07 per image.

The selected master is `../mascot/goose-face-paper-1024.png`. Re-export using
`python3 docs/brand/tools/refresh-icons.py` (Pillow required). App Store and
TestFlight updates require a new native build; no submission is part of this change.
