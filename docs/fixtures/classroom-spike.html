Synthetic tutor audio

This page plays alternating tones in the classroom tab. It never accesses your microphone.

This is a local capture test: no cloud upload, transcription, or processing charge. The extension's provisional limits are 60 minutes and 128 MiB across both channels.

Silent

  1. Use headphones. Close the microphone-check tab and return here.
  2. Click Schwanki's toolbar button on this tab, acknowledge recording consent, then press Record class.
  3. Say a short test sentence. Check that both audio meters move and the saved duration advances.
  4. Close only the extension panel for 30 seconds. Reopen it: duration must have advanced and the tutor tones must remain audible.
  5. Pause, wait five seconds, resume, say another sentence, and stop. Export saved audio parts for decoding.
