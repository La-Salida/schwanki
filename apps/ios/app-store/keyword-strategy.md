## Placement rationale

**Verified:** the product is a vocabulary-review companion with flashcards, a visual feed, spaced repetition, imported decks, audio and an offline cache. The native source supports language-tagged cards; language keywords do not promise built-in courses or a translated app interface.

**Medium confidence:** “vocab flashcards” belongs in the name because it explains the use case immediately. “Spaced repetition” explains the study method in the subtitle; “made visual” distinguishes the experience. The branded phrase “Doomscroll your vocab” leads the description, promotional text and first screenshot, where it can sell the experience without displacing useful category words.

These priority scores are editorial judgments. They use relevance (0–5) × 12 plus intended-user fit (0–5) × 8. They are not estimated search volumes, Apple difficulty scores, or the skill's composite volume model: no measured volume dataset is available.
