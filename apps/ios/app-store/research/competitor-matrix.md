## Positioning decision

**Medium confidence:** lead with vocabulary flashcards; use the illustrated swipe feed as the reason to choose Schwanki. Treat “Doomscroll your vocab” as conversion copy rather than an established search term. Spaced repetition explains the learning mechanism. Avoid generic homework, exam-answering, AI-tutor and built-in-course claims: the iPhone companion does not provide those flows.

**Verified scope:** iPhone review, sample feed, implemented deck import, offline cache and sync; creation/editing/document connections belong to the web. Native import remains gated on hosted migration and end-to-end acceptance before release.

**Unverified:** monthly keyword volume, search difficulty, organic ranking, conversion lift and download forecasts. No numerical estimates are supplied without an ASO-provider dataset. A low coverage count alone does not demonstrate demand or competitive whitespace.
